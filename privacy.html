Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 19, 2026

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

argotactical, llc ("we," "our," or "us") operates https://www.argotactical.com (the "Website"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data collection and the practices of third-party entities that interact with you when you visit our Website.

To ensure compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other US state privacy laws, this policy provides transparent disclosures regarding how data is handled.

1. No Direct Data Collection

We do not directly collect, harvest, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) or personal data from visitors to our Website.

  • No User Accounts: You are not required to create an account or log in to use our Website.
  • No Contact Forms: We do not host forms or text fields that request your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

2. Automated Data Collection by Third Parties

While we do not collect your data, your web browser automatically communicates information to third-party services and infrastructure necessary to display and run this Website. These external parties act as independent data controllers or processors:

A. Web Hosting Providers and Internet Service Providers (ISPs)

When you access our Website, the server hosting our files automatically logs standard web request data (often called "log files"). This process is routine for almost all websites. The data processed may include:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring/exit pages and URLs
  • Date and time of your visit

B. GitHub Pages

This Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. ("GitHub"). GitHub, as our hosting provider, may collect the technical request data described above and may set cookies or use similar technologies as described in its own policies.

  • No Third-Party Resources: This Website does not load fonts, scripts, analytics, or other resources from third-party servers. All fonts and files are served from the same host as the pages themselves.
  • You can learn how GitHub handles this data by reviewing the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not deploy proprietary tracking cookies. However, our hosting provider mentioned in Section 2 may deploy non-essential cookies for performance, security, or functional optimization.

You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your individual browser settings. Restricting cookies will not impact your ability to view or navigate our Website.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For visitors browsing from the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, the legal basis for third parties processing your technical data (such as IP addresses) is Legitimate Interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. This processing is strictly necessary to ensure technical connectivity, website security, and seamless server infrastructure performance.

5. Your Global Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographical location (including the EU/UK and US states like California, Virginia, Colorado, etc.), you are granted specific rights regarding your data under frameworks like the GDPR and CCPA:

  • Right to Know/Access: The right to request disclosure of what personal data is collected.
  • Right to Deletion/Erasure: The right to request that your personal data be deleted.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: The right to prevent the sale of your data or its use for targeted advertising.

How to Exercise Your Rights: Because argotactical, llc does not log, retain, or store any of your data, we possess no data to access, delete, or modify. To exercise your rights regarding data collected automatically by third parties, you must contact those specific third-party providers (such as your ISP or GitHub) directly.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our Website is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly interact with children. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly request or gather data from children under the age of 13 (US) or 16 (EU).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect structural changes to our Website or shifts in privacy legislation. Any updates will be published directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
