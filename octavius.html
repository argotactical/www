Meet the legend

You've likely noticed the octopus (you thought elephants were difficult to ignore!). He is Octavius, argo's AI operating system. He keeps the organization functioning behind the scenes, and like our argonauts, he ventures into complexity with confidence and curiosity.

Octavius is not a product, and we cant won't sell him (that would be a cruel thing to do to a client colleague). He is ubiquitous in everything we do as he manages travel, people, finance and operations.
